# Purpose

This document defines the core business concepts of the AI-Native Operations Platform.

It identifies:

- Core Business Entities
- Supporting Entities
- Value Objects
- Domain Services
- Relationships
- Ownership Boundaries
- Business Rules
- State Machines

The objective is to understand the business before designing the database.

This document acts as the bridge between System Architecture and Database Design.

---

# Domain Overview

The platform manages organizations and their financial operations.

Every activity within the system belongs to an Organization.

Users interact with organizations through membership, where permissions are determined by assigned roles.

Financial records are created, verified, stored, and reported within the boundaries of an organization.

---

# Domain Classification

The domain consists of five major categories.

- Core Entities
- Supporting Entities
- Value Objects
- Domain Services
- Generated Views

---

# 1. Core Entities

Core entities represent the primary business objects.

These objects have:

- Identity
- Lifecycle
- Relationships
- Persistent storage

## Organization

Represents an organization that owns financial records, members, budgets, and operational data.

---

## User

Represents a registered individual using the platform.

A user may belong to multiple organizations.

---

## Member

Represents the relationship between a User and an Organization.

Stores:

- Role
- Join Date
- Membership Status

---

## Expense

Represents money spent by an organization.

Stores:

- Amount
- Category
- Description
- Status
- Created By
- Approval Information

---

## Payment

Represents money received or paid.

May be linked to receipts and expenses.

---

## Receipt

Represents uploaded proof of payment.

Contains:

- File
- OCR Results
- Verification Status

---

## Budget

Represents allocated financial limits for an organization.

---

# 2. Supporting Entities

Supporting entities exist to improve system functionality.

---

## Notification

Represents messages delivered to users.

Examples:

- Expense Approved
- Member Invited
- Receipt Verified

---

## Audit Log

Stores important historical actions.

Example:

- Expense Created
- Budget Updated
- User Invited

---

## Invitation

Represents pending invitations sent to users before they join an organization.

---

# 3. Value Objects

Value objects describe properties.

They do not have independent identity.

---

## Role

Examples:

- Owner
- Admin
- Treasurer
- Member

---

## Expense Status

Examples:

- Draft
- Submitted
- Approved
- Rejected
- Archived

---

## Payment Status

Examples:

- Pending
- Verified
- Completed
- Failed

---

## Receipt Status

Examples:

- Uploaded
- OCR Processed
- Verified

---

## Money

Represents:

- Amount
- Currency

---

## Address (Future)

May be introduced if organizations require physical locations.

---

# 4. Domain Services

Domain services perform business operations.

They are not entities.

---

## OCR Service

Extracts structured information from receipts.

Input:

Receipt Image

Output:

Structured Receipt Data

---

## Report Service

Generates financial reports.

---

## Notification Service

Dispatches notifications.

---

## Authentication Service

Authenticates users.

---

## Authorization Service

Validates permissions.

---

# 5. Generated Views

Generated views do not own data.

They combine information from multiple entities.

---

## Dashboard

Displays:

- Budgets
- Expenses
- Payments
- Notifications

---

## Reports

Generated from financial records.

May optionally be exported.

# Business Rules

BR-001

Every Organization must have at least one Owner.

---

BR-002

A User may belong to multiple Organizations.

---

BR-003

A Member belongs to exactly one Organization.

---

BR-004

Roles are assigned to Members, not Users.

---

BR-005

Every Expense belongs to exactly one Organization.

---

BR-006

Every Payment belongs to exactly one Organization.

---

BR-007

OCR never modifies financial records automatically.

Users must verify extracted information before saving.

---

BR-008

Only authorized roles may approve financial records.

---

BR-009

Deleted financial records should be soft deleted.

---

BR-010

Every important financial action should generate an audit log.

# Domain Principles

The domain follows these principles:

- Organization is the tenant boundary.
- Business rules belong to the domain.
- Services perform actions.
- Entities own data.
- Value Objects describe data.
- Generated Views never own data.
- AI assists business workflows but does not replace human decisions.
